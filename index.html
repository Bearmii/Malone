Malone ain't playing a character — he's living one. By day, boots on and tools out, building shit that lasts. By night, the mic drops and the city knows his name.

No filter. No script. No off switch. Born from the dust of construction sites and raised on the energy of late nights, Malone is the collision of grind and release. When the work's done, the party starts. And when the party's done, he's already back at it.

Melbourne-raised. Unapologetic. Built different.
